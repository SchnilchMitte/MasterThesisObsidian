## Source discipline for the thesis

When a claim is specific to the project (port, host, path, topology, version, sampling rate), prefer the actual configuration or team documentation over generic product documentation.

## Networking fundamentals

For foundational Ethernet/IP/TCP/UDP concepts, use standards/textbooks or vendor-neutral networking references in the final thesis. The vault summaries are study notes and should not replace primary standards where a standards-level claim matters.
