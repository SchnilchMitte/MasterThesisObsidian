# Thesis Contribution Map

Potential contributions should be stated as outcomes, not just implementation tasks.

## Architecture contribution

A documented event-driven data architecture connecting heterogeneous network telemetry, flow data, topology/control state, an NDT, monitoring, and closed-loop control.

## Engineering contribution

A working prototype integrating selected data paths through Kafka with explicit topic, key, schema, retention, and failure-handling design.

## Experimental contribution

Measurements of synchronization quality and network/resource overhead under load, scaling, and failures.

## Operational contribution

A health/observability concept indicating whether the NDT's state is trustworthy and helping localize network vs pipeline problems.

## Practical deployment insight

Evidence showing how assumptions in an isolated/overprovisioned laboratory environment may change under constrained/shared infrastructure.
